Hi everyone, and thank you for playing the demo! Version 1.1.0 is now available. Here is what's new.

[image: brawl.gif]

NEW MODE: GOAT PATH

• A roguelite run across a map of matches, Elite teams, skill games and a shop, ending in a boss fight.
• One loss ends the run.
• Recruit legends and collect gear and badges along the way.
• Act 1 is playable in the demo.

ULTIMATES, REWORKED

Your Ultimate now depends on the Cores you pick during the match. Stack one kind, awaken it, or mix two for a hybrid.

[image: ultimate.gif]

LOOK AND FEEL

• New animations for passing, sprinting, shooting and goalkeeping.
• Redrawn pitches and shadows, and support for wide screens.
• Goal celebrations and a trophy room.

ALSO

• You can now rebind every key.
• The game is now available in Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Spanish and Japanese.

COMING NEXT

The full game is coming soon to Steam, with two more acts and online play.

Thanks again for your support. If you have feedback or run into a bug, please let us know in the comments!
