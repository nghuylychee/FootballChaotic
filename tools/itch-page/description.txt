[image: logo.png]

Football, but the tackle button is a punch. Punch the ball carrier, send defenders flying into the wall, and build a ridiculous playstyle from mid-match upgrades.

[image: h-features.png]

• No tackles, only fists. A quick punch to knock the ball loose, or a charged dropkick that launches people across the pitch.
• 53 Core upgrades. Pick a card after goals and stack an archetype: runner, playmaker, striker, brawler, launcher, trickster, iron, chaos.
• 7 ultimates with anime cut-ins: Meteor Shot, Hundred Fists, Clone Army, Titan…
• Main Path campaign: climb 10 areas, from a village pitch to the World Stage.
• Dress your player: 80 costume pieces (hats, faces, shoes, trail effects) from gacha boxes.
• Train your stats with drill cards every level.
• Online 1v1 with a room code.

[image: h-cores.png]

After every goal, both teams pick one of three Cores. Stack two, three or four of the same archetype to unlock bonuses. Hold two of one style and that style's ultimate can show up in your picks. Every match plays out differently.

[image: h-controls.png]

Arrow keys — Move
E (hold) — Sprint
S — Quick pass
W — Through ball
A — Lob pass · defending: dropkick
D — Shoot (hold to charge) · defending: punch
Z — Skill move / dash
X — Ultimate (when full)
1 2 3 — Pick a Core
Esc — Pause

Gamepads work too. Click the game first so it gets your key presses.

[image: h-online.png]

Create a room, send the 7-letter code to a friend, and play 1v1.

[image: h-download.png]

The browser version saves your progress in this browser. Clearing site data or switching browsers starts you over. The Windows download keeps saves in a file on your PC.
