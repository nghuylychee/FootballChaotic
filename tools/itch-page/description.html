Street Football Chaos is a football roguelite: start with a scrappy street squad, draft a new power with every goal, and become the GOATs of street football. Every run brings a new map, a new squad and a new build.

Punch the ball off the carrier, dropkick the opponent, and turn every loose ball into a brawl. Matches are fast 3v3 chaos, and every team you meet brings its own house rule to make it messier!

Cores are powers that bend the match your way. Shooting, passing, punching, speed: each Core powers up one part of your game, so every pick decides how you beat the other team, and one good pick can turn around a match you were losing.

Pick three Cores of the same kind and you unlock their Ultimate, a super move only that build can pull off. 37 Ultimates to discover!

Choose your way across the map: matches, tougher Elite teams, skill games, a shop and surprise events, with a boss waiting at the end. Lose once and the run is over, so pick your fights.

Pull off a legend's signature move in his trial and that parody football star joins your squad, flaw and all. Kit your players out with gear, and collect badges that bend the rules for the whole team, like a one-goal head start or a VAR check on the goals you concede.

The game works with keyboard and gamepad (Xbox and PlayStation controllers).
