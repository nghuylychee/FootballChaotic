3v3 street football with no referee. To win the ball back, you punch whoever has it.

  • Your tackle is a punch. Punch the ball carrier to knock the ball loose, or dropkick them into the wall.
  • No referee. No fouls, no cards, no whistle. Every loose ball turns into a brawl.
  • Power up your team after every goal. Pick one of three Cores, upgrades that last the rest of the match: faster sprints, harder shots, heavier punches. 53 Cores in all, 7 of them ultimates.
  • Main Path campaign: climb 10 areas, from a village pitch to the World Stage.
  • Dress your player: 80 costume pieces (hats, faces, shoes, trail effects), unlocked with gold you earn in matches.
  • Level up your player: every level, pick one of three stat boosts: pace, shooting, passing, dribbling, fighting or goalkeeping.
  • Online 1v1 with a room code.

After every goal, both teams pick one of three Cores. Stack two, three or four of the same archetype to unlock bonuses. Hold two of one style and that style's ultimate can show up in your picks.
