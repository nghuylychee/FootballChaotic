Hi everyone! Version 1.1.2 of the demo is out. This one is all about how a match feels.

[image: match-1.1.2.gif]

BIGGER PITCH, FASTER MATCHES

• The pitch is bigger, and players are drawn larger and in more detail.
• Matches play faster: quicker runs, passes and shots.
• Taller goals, and the net shakes where the ball hits it.
• The ball now glides to your feet when you pick it up, and loose balls are easier to control.

SMARTER AI

• The AI keeper stays in goal and only rushes out when it has to.
• Defenders step up behind the play when their team attacks.
• Teammates make overlapping runs down the wing.
• Opponents press harder, so expect more tackles.

GOAT PATH

• Build Your Squad is now laid out on a pitch: click or drag players into position.
• The gear reward screen is redesigned and much easier to read.

LOOK AND FEEL

• New sprint animations.
• Knocked-down players now get back up step by step.
• Menus and reward screens appear faster, and you can skip their animations.

FIXES

• Fixed a bug that could freeze the game.
• Fixed a frame rate drop.
• Opponents no longer block the keeper's kick from his hands.

Thanks for playing! If you have feedback or run into a bug, please let us know in the comments.
