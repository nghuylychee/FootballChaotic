# Street Football Chaos — brag plan

**What it is:** a 2v2 pixel-art street football game in the browser, where tackling is replaced by punching and dropkicking, and mid-match "Core" upgrades (augments, LoL Arena style) turn matches into anime fights.
**Who for:** indie/game-dev Twitter + anyone who likes couch-chaos games; this clip is a build-in-public update.
**What sets it apart:** no tackles, just punches and hard kicks that send people into walls; 53 Cores including 7 ultimates with manga cut-ins; zero asset files, so every sprite, costume and sound is drawn or synthesized in code; no build step.
**Funniest true claim:** "It's football." The next second: a dropkick sends the ball carrier into the wall ("BONK!").
**Visual hook:** the real game, frame-perfect, at 3× pixel scale: a normal attack, then a dropkick.
**Tone:** `chaotic`: fast cuts, flash/zoom transitions, ALL CAPS, Press Start 2P (the game's own font), the game's palette (#ff3d5a red, #ffe14f gold, #7fe7ff cyan, ink #140c16).
**Share caption:** "Street Football Chaos: 2v2 pixel street football where the tackle button is a dropkick."

## Sound
One chiptune track (A minor, 160 BPM, square/pulse lead like the game's WebAudio SFX, saw bass, 8-bit drums). Impacts are low booms tuned to A, and they sit under the music. They land on the game's own logged hit/kick/goal events and on every cut. There's a riser into the logo slam, and a stop-time hit on the dropkick.
