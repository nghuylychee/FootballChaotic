# Street Football Chaos: brag plan (v2)

**What it is:** a 2v2 pixel-art street football game in the browser, where tackling is replaced by punching and dropkicking, and mid-match "Core" upgrades (augments, LoL Arena style) turn matches into anime fights.
**Who for:** indie/game-dev audiences; this clip is a build-in-public teaser.
**What sets it apart:** no tackles, only punches and hard kicks that send people into walls; 53 Cores to build a playstyle, including 7 ultimates with manga cut-ins; gacha boxes full of cosmetics to dress your player.
**Hook:** "It's football." The next second, a dropkick sends the ball carrier into the wall ("CRACK!").
**Tone:** `chaotic`: fast cuts, flash/zoom transitions, ALL CAPS, Press Start 2P (the game's own font), game palette (#ff3d5a red, #ffe14f gold, #7fe7ff cyan, ink #140c16).
**Game text:** all in-game text is English as of this version (UI, Core cards, cut-ins, comic callouts).

## Sound
One chiptune track (A minor, 160 BPM, square/pulse lead, bass, 8-bit drums). Impacts are low booms tuned to A, sitting under the music and landing on the game's own logged hit/kick/goal events and on every cut. Riser into the logo, stop-time hit on the dropkick, reel ticks from the real gacha spin, a small chime on the reveal and soft pops on each costume swap.
