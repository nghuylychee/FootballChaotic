## Patterns

1. **Item first, then who** (PokeRogue, Monster Train 2, TFT, Super Auto Pets) vs **who first, then item** (EA FC, Mario Strikers, the Darkest Dungeon sheet). Item-first suits a reward that pops up after a match. Who-first suits tuning a loadout at leisure, but costs more steps (EA FC ≈ 6).
2. **Assign now vs park it for later.** Assign now: PokeRogue, Monster Train, Super Auto Pets. Park it: Wildfrost, Darkest Dungeon, Into the Breach, TFT bench, FTL cargo. Parking makes the reward screen lighter, but parked items do nothing until equipped, and players forget them (the Into the Breach thread).
3. **Eligibility shown on each target before you pick** (PokeRogue's "Able/Not able/Learned" replaces the HP bar). The alternative is rejecting after you pick ("has too many of this item!"). Pre-marking costs space on each target; rejecting costs a wasted press.
4. **Show the result on the target, not on the offer card.** Monster Train 2 shows the upgraded unit card, TFT pops up the combined item, Mario Strikers moves the character's stats. The player sees the before → after for the one member they're about to choose. It needs a hover or focus state, which a gamepad has to reach.
5. **Full slot handled as a swap that keeps the old item** (Darkest Dungeon: the old trinket goes back to inventory) vs **replace and lose it** (Hades, Super Auto Pets' one perk, Slay the Spire 2 discard-or-lose). Swap-and-keep removes the fear of a wrong choice but needs storage. Replace-and-lose is one press but needs a clear warning.
6. **Overflow storage instead of a forced choice** (FTL cargo, Fire Emblem convoy, Darkest Dungeon inventory). No blocking "pick one to drop" step, but you add an inventory to manage.
7. **Small, clear caps:** Super Auto Pets 1 perk, Hades 1 per slot, Monster Train 2, Darkest Dungeon 2, TFT 3, Wildfrost 3. Games where items are permanent (Wildfrost, Monster Train, TFT combined items) are the ones that invest in previews; Darkest Dungeon, where you can swap back, needs none.
8. **No "who" step at all:** Super Auto Pets' random/all-pet foods and Brotato's single character. The fastest flow, but no choice of target.

Not checked, worth a look in-game: the Hades replacement card, Slay the Spire 1's smith screen and full-potion flow, and the Wildfrost / Super Auto Pets controls.
