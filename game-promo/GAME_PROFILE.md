## Name
**Street Football Chaos** (package.json `productName`). High.
Search warning: "Street Football" (Hede, 2021) and "Streets of Chaos" are different games on Steam with similar names. Always use the full name in titles, tags and descriptions.

## Genre
Top-down 2.5D pixel-art arcade street football, **3v3**, with fighting instead of tackles, plus a roguelite run mode (GOAT PATH, Slay-the-Spire-style map). High.

## Core loop
- **In a match:** dribble, pass and shoot. To win the ball back you **punch** the carrier or **dropkick** them (no referee, no fouls). Before kickoff and after goals, each team **drafts 1 of 3 Cores** (mid-match upgrades). 3 Cores of one school unlock that school's **Ultimate**, 4 awaken it, and 2 + 2 of two schools gives a hybrid Ultimate, each with a manga cut-in. High.
- **Main mode, GOAT PATH:** a 3-act run on a branching map (matches, Elite teams, skill games, shop, locker room, ??? events, bosses). Lose one match and the run is over. You build a squad of 3 and collect BADGE items. Solo or online co-op. High.
- **Side modes:** Ranked (Elo, matchmaking), Private Match (room codes, up to 6 players). High.
- **Meta:** gold, gacha boxes, 104 cosmetic items, 20 goal celebrations, Core unlocks from run progress. High.

## Most visually exciting moments (for clips)
1. **Dropkick into the wall**: the ball carrier flies into the wall with a comic impact word (CRACK! / POW!). Understandable with no language. High.
2. **Ultimate cut-ins**: manga cut-in, then a big on-pitch effect (e.g. Meteor Shot, Hundred Fists, Clone Army, Chicken Ray turning opponents into chickens). High.
3. **Opponent pitch rules as gags**: the uncles throw a plastic stool, a pigeon gang bombs the pitch, giant chopsticks grab a player, a corrupt VAR. Med (rules exist in config; this set was picked for earlier clips).
4. **Goal celebration with camera zoom-in** (20 moves, teammates jog over). High.
5. **Clutch finish**: last-second goal, e.g. Clone Army. Med.

## USP (one sentence)
3v3 pixel street football with no referee: your tackle is a punch, and every goal lets you draft upgrades that stack into anime Ultimates. Med (our wording, built from the itch draft + design docs).
